# Merry Scratch — 简历项目描述

## 中文版（推荐）

**Merry Scratch** | 创始人 & 独立开发者 | 2026.06 - 至今

从零设计并独立开发了一款节日社交刮刮乐 PWA 应用。用户可定制奖品并生成专属链接分享给朋友，接收方通过刮开圣诞树和银色圆片的交互方式获得惊喜。产品覆盖赠送者配置、被赠者刮奖、裂变传播完整闭环。技术栈：Vanilla JS + Canvas + 腾讯云 CloudBase。上线后通过微信朋友圈传播，验证了"轻量社交送礼"的产品模式。

## 英文版

**Merry Scratch** | Founder & Independent Developer | Jun 2026 - Present

Designed and built a social scratch-off PWA from scratch. Senders customize prizes and share unique links; receivers scratch to reveal surprise gifts on a Christmas tree. Features full gift configuration flow, Canvas-based scratch interactions, real-time progress tracking, and viral sharing mechanics. Tech stack: Vanilla JS, Canvas API, Tencent CloudBase. Launched via WeChat social sharing.
